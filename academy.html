Frequently asked questions

What is the GEI Academy for?

It is the learner experience for GEI. The Academy helps users study the method step by step and apply it in practice.

Complete lessons and use the course path. Your progress and quiz results update your Academy dashboard.

What comes after knowledge?

After knowledge comes reasoning, learning, and creation. That is where GEI becomes a living practice instead of just information.
