Meet your guide

Every drop has a job. You just have to figure out which one.

Baxter the Beaver is a water engineer of the old school — the one that still works. He learned his craft building dams, canal networks, and water wheels across three generations. He'll meet you at the spring, walk you through your first canal, and warn you about flooding before it's too late.

He doesn't lecture. He points. He builds. He laughs when the village floods — because you'll fix it, and you'll remember why the gate mattered.
